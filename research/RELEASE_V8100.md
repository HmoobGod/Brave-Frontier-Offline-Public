The original Brave Frontier Global community mechanics source records that **Battle Crystals (BC)** use a nominal **35% base chance per *drop check***, whereas **Heart Crystals (HC)** use **10% base chance per *individual hit***. BC drop-check counts are *separate* from attack hit counts. The source further describes an **overkill ×2 drop-rate modifier** and an integer RNG in 0–100 inclusive with a <= final-rate threshold. See:
- <https://bravefrontierglobal.fandom.com/wiki/Drop_Rate>
- <https://bravefrontierglobal.fandom.com/wiki/Drop_Rate_Boost>

Unlike full native skill data, the user's 2022 `F_UNIT` master has 71 valid source normal-attack hit frame/weight records, already bundled by v7.00 in `BFOriginalHitSequenceV700`. v81 adds a **pure battle-crystal / heart-crystal calculation core**, bound to those exact source hit records. It validates that weights sum to 100 and does **not** synthesize a hit count for missing or malformed unit #10023. It computes expected base HC from documented normal hits and allows deterministic or seeded rolls at authentic nominal BC/HC percentages, 0–100 inclusive RNG and overkill scaling. BC roll requires an **explicit separate drop-check count** and returns null when missing; the 71 source hit records never silently stand in for drop-check data.
